# Write each rule title as one complete claim with a body that explains its judgment.

Titles appear without bodies in generated guides, so write a complete, specific claim that tells a reader what applies. Give each rule one independently useful claim rather than a topic label or a checklist of unrelated obligations.

Bodies are freeform Markdown. Include the rationale, scope, exceptions, evidence, and examples needed to apply the claim. Do not impose a section template when a short paragraph is sufficient. Rules guide judgment; mechanical checks validate file structure and projections, not semantic compliance.
